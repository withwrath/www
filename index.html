what we're doing

Most labs are building models to talk to people. We're building models to act on networks — to find the way in, understand what they've found, and decide what to do next, without a human narrating every step.

Cyber operations are a narrow domain with a wide surface: reconnaissance, exploit development, lateral movement, persistence, cleanup. We think a model purpose-built for that domain, rather than adapted to it, ends up somewhere general-purpose systems don't reach.
