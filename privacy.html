privacy policy

01 — information we collect

Information you provide

When you join the wrath labs waitlist, we may collect:

  • your email address;
  • information you voluntarily include in communications with us; and
  • other information you choose to provide.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through our website or waitlist.

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, such as:

  • IP address;
  • browser and device type;
  • operating system;
  • pages or resources accessed;
  • referring website;
  • approximate location derived from technical information; and
  • date and time of access.

We may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for the operation, security, or functionality of the website.

02 — how we use information

We may use personal information to:

  • operate and maintain our website;
  • manage our waitlist;
  • send updates about wrath labs, our models, products, and services;
  • respond to inquiries;
  • understand website usage and improve our website;
  • maintain security and prevent abuse; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use your email address to send unrelated third-party marketing.

03 — waitlist communications

If you provide your email address through our waitlist, we may use it to contact you about hosted inference access, product availability, research updates, or other wrath labs developments.

You may unsubscribe from non-essential communications at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions included in the communication or by contacting us.

04 — sharing information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the website and our services, such as hosting, email delivery, analytics, security, and infrastructure providers.

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide services to us and subject to appropriate contractual or other protections.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request;
  • protect the rights, property, or safety of wrath labs, our users, or others;
  • investigate fraud, security issues, or abuse; or
  • facilitate a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar corporate transaction.

We do not sell your personal information.

05 — data retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including maintaining the waitlist and providing communications you have requested.

We may retain certain information for longer where required by law, necessary to resolve disputes, or needed to enforce our agreements.

06 — international transfers

Wrath Labs and our service providers may process information in countries other than the country in which you live.

Where required by applicable law, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers of personal information.

07 — your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to:

  • request access to personal information we hold about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate information;
  • request deletion of your personal information;
  • request restriction of certain processing;
  • object to certain processing;
  • request portability of information you have provided; and
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise an applicable privacy right, contact us using the information below. We may need to verify your request before completing it.

08 — cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies.

Cookies may be used for purposes such as maintaining website functionality, understanding how the website is used, and protecting the website from abuse.

You can generally control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

09 — third-party services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services, including services such as GitHub and Hugging Face.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites. Their own privacy policies apply when you interact with those services.

10 — security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11 — children's privacy

Our website is not directed toward children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

12 — changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. The updated policy will become effective when posted unless otherwise stated.

13 — contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise a privacy right, please contact wrath labs through the contact information provided on our website.
